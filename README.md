I help companies across the DACH region and Europe **untangle their data chaos** and build clear, scalable data systems — end-to-end Lakehouse platforms covering ingestion, transformation, orchestration, governance, and FinOps. Deep expertise in **Databricks, Delta Lake, Medallion architectures, Unity Catalog, PySpark**, and infrastructure-as-code. Recent engagements include energy-industry and SaaS clients running millions of records per day on AWS and Azure.

### What I do at Neoinsights

- **Data Architecture** — Medallion Lakehouses, governance with Unity Catalog, schema evolution and SCD design
- **Data Engineering** — PySpark, Spark Structured Streaming, Delta Lake, Iceberg, idempotent and incremental pipelines
- **AI Engineering** — production RAG systems and agentic workflows with LangChain, LangGraph, and the OpenAI Agents SDK
- **Platform & FinOps** — Terraform IaC, CI/CD, observability, workload tuning that has delivered 15–25% cost reductions on real engagements
